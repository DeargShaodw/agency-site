Our Story

Vermilion Code grew out of a simple frustration — most small businesses either can't afford an agency or get burned by cheap overseas work that disappears after delivery. There's a gap in the middle where a skilled local developer who actually cares about your outcome can do serious work at a fair price.

That's the gap we fill. Every site is hand-coded from scratch — no drag-and-drop builders, no bloated templates, no shortcuts that break six months later. We're based in the San Fernando Valley and we serve clients locally and remotely.
