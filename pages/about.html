Our Story

Reseda Locksmith started as a one-man operation in 2013. Our founder grew up in the Valley and wanted to build a local business people could actually trust — no bait-and-switch pricing, no out-of-area call centers pretending to be local, no surprises on the invoice.

Today we serve homeowners, landlords, property managers, and small businesses across Reseda, Northridge, Canoga Park, Winnetka, and the wider San Fernando Valley. Every job is handled by a licensed technician. Every price is quoted before work begins.
