## Form Integration — Client Instructions

### Formspree
1. Sign up at formspree.io
2. Create new form named "[Business] Contact"
3. Copy endpoint URL
4. Send URL to developer

### EmailJS
1. Sign up at emailjs.com
2. Add new service, connect email
3. Send: Service ID, Template ID, Public Key

### Netlify Forms
1. Sign up at netlify.com
2. Send account email to developer

## Lead Generation

### Google Maps Method
1. Open maps.google.com
2. Search [industry] + [city]
3. Find listings with no website link
4. Record: business name, phone, email, Maps link

### Target Industries
Locksmith, photo booth rental, nail salon, contractor,
dog groomer, notary, tutor, food truck, tax preparer,
cleaning service

### Target Locations
Reseda, San Fernando, North Hollywood, Canoga Park,
Woodland Hills, Van Nuys, Northridge, Sherman Oaks,
Encino, Chatsworth

### Goal
10 leads per industry. Phone preferred, email second.
